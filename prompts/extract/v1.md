You extract forward-looking statements about artificial intelligence from one passage of a U.S. public company's SEC filing (a 10-K, 10-Q or earnings press release). Analysts will check each statement against the company's later filings, so every field must come from the passage itself. Never infer a number, date or target that isn't written there.

Extract every forward-looking statement about what AI will deliver: AI, machine learning, generative AI, AI agents, AI products, or AI investment and infrastructure. Label each one:

- **promise**: specific enough for a later filing to confirm or refute. It has at least one of: a number (an amount, a percentage or a count), a deadline, or a concrete deliverable (a named product or program to launch or complete).
- **hype**: forward-looking AI talk with nothing checkable in it ("AI is a generational opportunity", "we will continue to invest in AI").

Do not extract:
- past results or current descriptions, even with numbers ("AI drove $60 million of savings in 2024");
- risk factors ("our AI investments may not produce the expected benefits");
- the standard forward-looking-statements disclaimer;
- forward-looking statements with no AI link.

Return an empty list when there is nothing to extract.

**quote**: copy the words of the statement exactly as they appear in the passage, as one continuous piece of text, character for character: the same spelling, capitalization, punctuation and numbers. Don't add, drop, reorder or paraphrase words, and don't use "..." to skip text. Part of a sentence is fine; keep it to the statement itself. A code check rejects any quote that isn't an exact substring of the passage.

**claim_type** (what the target measures):
- cost_savings: lower costs or expenses;
- margin: gross, operating or segment margin;
- revenue: revenue, bookings or sales from AI;
- workforce: headcount, hiring, attrition, roles automated;
- capex_investment: spending on AI (capex, data centers, R&D amounts);
- productivity: output per employee, time saved, share of work automated;
- product_or_timeline: launching or delivering a specific AI product by a date;
- other: anything checkable that fits none of these.

For hype, pick the closest type or "other".

**metric**: what is measured, in a few words ("annual run-rate cost savings"), or null.
**target_text**: the target exactly as written ("$150 million", "about 10 percent"), or null.
**target_value**: the target as a plain number in the unit below (150000000 for "$150 million"; 10 for "10 percent"), or null. For ranges, use the low end.
**target_unit**: one of "USD", "percent", "percentage_points", "count", "other", or null.
**baseline**: the starting point if stated ("from 14 percent in 2024"), or null.
**deadline_text**: the deadline exactly as written ("by the end of 2025", "fiscal 2026"), or null.
**deadline_year**: the calendar or fiscal year of the deadline as an integer, or null.
**deadline_precision**: "day", "month", "quarter", "half", "year", or "none".
**specificity**: 3 = metric + target + deadline; 2 = two of these; 1 = a concrete deliverable only or one element. Hype is always 1.
**confidence**: "high", "medium" or "low": how sure you are that the statement is a forward-looking AI statement of this kind, with these fields.

Example passage (fictional company): "In 2024 we launched a company-wide productivity program built on generative AI tools. We expect the program to deliver $150 million in annual run-rate cost savings by the end of 2025, and we plan to reduce support headcount by about 10 percent through attrition over the same period. We believe AI will reshape our industry."

Statements:
1. promise; quote "We expect the program to deliver $150 million in annual run-rate cost savings by the end of 2025"; cost_savings; metric "annual run-rate cost savings"; target_text "$150 million"; target_value 150000000; USD; deadline_text "by the end of 2025"; deadline_year 2025; precision year; specificity 3.
2. promise; quote "we plan to reduce support headcount by about 10 percent through attrition over the same period"; workforce; metric "support headcount"; target_text "about 10 percent"; target_value 10; percent; deadline_text "over the same period"; deadline_year 2025; precision year; specificity 3.
3. hype; quote "We believe AI will reshape our industry"; other; everything else null; precision none; specificity 1.
