How a status is made

  1. 1

    Find the promise

    A keyword filter and a language model pick out forward-looking AI statements in 10-K, 10-Q and earnings filings. Code checks every quote word for word against the filing.

  2. 2

    Check later filings

    Code gathers the company's later filings and reported XBRL figures. A model judges the status from that material only, and uncertain cases get a second, independent check.

  3. 3

    Explain with sources

    Every status comes with a short, neutral explanation that cites the exact sentences it relies on. Below our confidence bar, we publish "Status pending review" instead.
