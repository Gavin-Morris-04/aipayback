# 5-minute demo script (live or recorded)

**Audience:** a design partner or a likely user (fund analyst, IR/compliance lead, journalist). **Goal:** they see one real promise traced from the filing to its status, see why they can trust it, and say what they'd use it for.

**Before you start:** open the live site, one company page you know well (pick one with a Delivered or On track promise and one "Status pending review"), the promise's SEC filing in another tab, and the Methodology page. Use the partner's own company or holding if it's covered.

| Time | Show | Say (in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Home page and leaderboard | "Since 2023, companies have made specific AI promises in their SEC filings. We track `[[N]]` of them across the S&P 100 and check each against what the company's later filings report." |
| 0:30–1:30 | One promise page | "Here's the promise, word for word from the 10-K. Code checks that quote against the filing. The status is On track, and the explanation cites the exact sentence in the later 10-Q, here [click the citation, then the SEC link]." |
| 1:30–2:15 | The reported-figures table | "Every number is from the company's XBRL data. The model never does arithmetic; code does." |
| 2:15–3:00 | A "Status pending review" item | "This is what we do when we're not sure: we show the quote and no status. A wrong verdict about a real company is the worst thing we could publish, so negative statuses need two independent checks that agree." |
| 3:00–3:45 | Methodology page, accuracy table | "We measure ourselves against hand labels by our finance lead, on companies held out from tuning: `[[F1]]` on finding promises, `[[precision]]` on published statuses." |
| 3:45–4:30 | Their workflow | "You said you spend about `[[their minutes]]` minutes checking a claim like this. What would you want next: alerts on your watchlist, an Excel export, or a benchmark against peers?" |
| 4:30–5:00 | Close | "Can we measure this on two of your real questions, one your usual way and one with AIPayback?" (the ROI plan) |

## Shot list for a recorded version (about 2 minutes)

1. Title card: "Did the AI promises pay off?" (3 s)
2. Scroll the leaderboard (5 s)
3. Click into one company, then one promise (10 s)
4. Zoom on the verbatim quote; split-screen with the SEC filing at the same sentence (10 s)
5. Click a citation: the cited sentence highlights (10 s)
6. Reported-figures table, with a callout "arithmetic by code" (8 s)
7. A "Status pending review" item, with a callout "no guesses" (8 s)
8. Methodology accuracy table (8 s)
9. End card: site URL, "Informational only. Not investment advice." (4 s)

**Rules:** real data only, no mocked screens. If a number on screen changes before publishing, re-record that shot.
