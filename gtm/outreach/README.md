# Design-partner outreach

**Goal:** 3–5 design partners: real people who will spend 30 minutes telling us how they research companies' AI claims today, then try AIPayback and tell us what's missing. We're **not selling** yet; we're learning.

**Who to contact** (one file each, with a LinkedIn note, a LinkedIn follow-up and an email):

| File | Who | Why they might care |
|---|---|---|
| [student_fund.md](student_fund.md) | Student investment fund members and portfolio managers | They write investment memos and need to check management claims fast |
| [professor.md](professor.md) | Finance and accounting professors | Disclosure quality is a research and teaching topic |
| [wealth_advisor.md](wealth_advisor.md) | Wealth advisors and RIAs | Clients ask "is the AI story real?" about their holdings |
| [journalist.md](journalist.md) | Student and business journalists | Fact-checking corporate AI claims is a story |
| [ir_compliance.md](ir_compliance.md) | Investor-relations, compliance and securities-law people | The SEC is scrutinizing AI disclosures; they need to know how their claims read next to peers' |

## Rules that keep this honest

- **Only claim what's true today.** Right now that's: "we download every 10-K, 10-Q and earnings release since 2024 for a pilot set of large companies and find every passage where they talk about AI." Accuracy numbers can go in messages only after Phase 2 measures them.
- Never name a company's claim as broken or false in outreach. That's the rubric's job, with citations.
- Personalize the first line every time: one specific thing about them (a fund's recent pitch, a professor's paper, an article). Connection notes are written at about 185–192 characters with the placeholders, so recheck the length after filling in names and keep it under 200.
- Ask for **20–30 minutes to learn**, not to demo.

## Cadence

1. Send the LinkedIn note (or the email when you have an address).
2. No reply after 4–5 days: send one follow-up (the second message in each file).
3. Still nothing: stop. Don't send a third message.
4. Log everything in [tracker.csv](tracker.csv) (opens in Excel or Sheets).

**Target:** about 25 messages to get 5–8 calls to get 3–5 partners. Book calls with the guide in [../discovery/GUIDE.md](../discovery/GUIDE.md).
