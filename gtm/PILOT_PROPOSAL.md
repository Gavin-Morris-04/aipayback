## What we'll deliver

An **AI Disclosure Benchmark** for `[[company]]` against `[[3–5 named peers]]`:
1. Every forward-looking AI statement in each company's 2024–2025 SEC filings, split into checkable promises and general AI language.
2. For each promise: the verbatim quote, the status from later filings (Delivered, On track, No evidence yet, Walked back), and citations to both, with SEC links.
3. A one-page comparison: how specific each company's AI language is, and how much of it later filings support.
4. An Excel workbook of everything above, plus a 30-minute walkthrough call.

**What it is not:** legal advice, investment advice, or an opinion on any company's disclosure controls. It reports what the filings say.
